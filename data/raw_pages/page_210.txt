188         CONSTITUTION OF PAKISTAN
     Wherein shall be guaranteed fundamental rights includingequality of status, of opportunity and before law, social, economicand political justice, and freedom of thought, expression, belief,faith, worship and association, subject to law and public morality;
     Wherein adequate provision shall be made to safeguard thelegitimate interests of minorities and backward and depressedclasses;
     Wherein the independence of the Judiciary shall be fullysecured;
     Wherein the integrity of the territories of the Federation, itsindependence and all its rights including its sovereign rights onland, sea and air shall be safeguarded;
    So that the people of Pakistan may prosper and attain theirrightful and honoured place amongst the nations of the World andmake  their  full  contribution towards  international peace andprogress and happiness of humanity.]
