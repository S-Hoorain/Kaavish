                  CONSTITUTION OF PAKISTAN
                   PREFACE
       This Fourteenth Edition of the Constitution of the Islamic Republicof Pakistan, 1973 incorporates all amendments made till date. It reflects thecontinuing evolution of Pakistan’s constitutional framework.
       In  pursuit  of  greater  accessibility and transparency  in  theadministration of justice, the Law and Justice Division has also developeda comprehensive digital database of the Federal Laws of Pakistan, titled“The Pakistan Code.” This initiative provides seamless access to the lawsof Pakistan both domestically and internationally through a dedicatedwebsite and user-friendly mobile applications now available on the AppleApp  Store,  Google  Play  Store  and  the   official  web  portalwww.pakistancode.gov.pk. The Pakistan Code represents a major steptowards modernising  legal  dissemination and  facilitating  citizens,practitioners, and institutions in obtaining authentic legal references withease.
